# Dune.nvim Themes for VS Code

Four colour themes from the Dune universe, each house rendered in the
light of its own world — the VS Code port of the
[dune.nvim](https://github.com/jonestristand/dune.nvim) colourscheme
series for Neovim. One violet (`#A188CC`) sits on types and classes in
every palette, because the Sisterhood operates in every corner of the
Imperium.

Install the extension, then `Preferences: Color Theme` → pick a house.
The integrated terminal is styled too: its ANSI slots share one mapping
with Neovim's `:terminal` and the terminal ports in the main repository,
so every port agrees on what red means.

See every house side by side on the showcase site:
[dune-nvim.tdjones.ca](https://dune-nvim.tdjones.ca).

## Dune Atreides — the shoreline of two worlds

Caladan's cold sea against the hot sand of Arrakis: spice-orange
keywords, melange-gold constants, and sea-teal strings over ink-dark
water. The duke's house, and the flagship of the series.

<img src="https://dune-nvim.tdjones.ca/vscode/dune-atreides.png" alt="Dune Atreides" width="620"/>

## Dune Harkonnen — the black sun

Giedi Prime permits no colour it does not need. Keywords are carried by
weight, not hue — bold glare on machine shadow — and the furnace ember
is rationed out to errors alone. Coolant blue is an industrial
concession, not a mercy.

<img src="https://dune-nvim.tdjones.ca/vscode/dune-harkonnen.png" alt="Dune Harkonnen" width="620"/>

## Dune Corrino — the golden lion throne

Kaitain: porphyry and gold. The metal owns the frame — bold throne-gold
keywords, gilt-leaf operators and punctuation — while the court jewels
are handed out precisely: emerald strings, sapphire functions, ruby
errors.

<img src="https://dune-nvim.tdjones.ca/vscode/dune-corrino.png" alt="Dune Corrino" width="620"/>

## Dune Fremen — the desert hides its life

The sietch: carved stone and hoarded water. Moonlit sand over the open
night, wormsign where something has gone wrong, and the two colours the
desert keeps hidden — prophecy-green strings and the blue of Ibad on
functions, worn by those who can afford the water of strangers.

<img src="https://dune-nvim.tdjones.ca/vscode/dune-fremen.png" alt="Dune Fremen" width="620"/>

## Dune Tleilaxu

There is no fifth theme. Do not select Dune Tleilaxu. It does not
exist, and it will work perfectly: bone-wax keywords over vat glass,
axlotl-green strings, and every call site answered by its own ghola —
originals declare; gholas repeat.

## The violet

`#A188CC`, unchanged in every house: sisterhoodViolet in four palettes,
reverendViolet in the open desert. It marks types and classes wherever
you go, because the Sisterhood has agents everywhere — and so does your
type checker.
